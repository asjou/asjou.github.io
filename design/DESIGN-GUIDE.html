01Intent

A records index with a noir surface

What the site is. A public index of FCC equipment-authorization records: public notices, orders to show cause, and Covered List actions. Every entry is a real document with a date and a docket number. The page lists them newest first, leads with the newest, and sends the reader to the source.

What the design does. It keeps the discipline of a printed newspaper of record and adds one layer of atmosphere: light through venetian blinds behind the masthead, a lamp over the newsletter, fog at the foot of the page. Everything between those surfaces is type, rules, and space.

Four principles. Every choice in this guide follows from them.

  • Atmosphere lives on surfaces, not in ornament. Two generated plates and a cut-metal wordmark carry the noir. No dinkus, no seal, no corner brackets, no glyphs on tags.
  • One lead, then equals. The newest record is one size up with a three-line summary and a rule beneath it. Every record after it sits at one size in date order.
  • Jade in the rules, never on a control. Jade marks structure: hairlines, the active section, link hover. The one button is bone. Vermilion marks enforcement risk and nothing else.
  • Three greys, three jobs. Ink for titles, tags, and dates. Dim for summaries and rail text. Ash for labels, dockets, and the colophon.
02Colour

Cool ink on neutral black, jade in the rules

The ground is a neutral near-black. Ink is a cool white. Between them sit two greys with fixed jobs. Jade appears only as hairlines, the active navigation marker with its glow, and link hover. Vermilion appears only on the Bad Labs tag and its count in the rail. No green, gold, or vermilion on any control.

Hairlines

All rules are jade at reduced opacity. Three strengths, used by role.

TokenValueWhere

hair2jade at 34%Rule under the lead record, rail section heads

Rail list rows use ink at 6% instead, so the rail sits quieter than the ledger.

03Plates

Two generated surfaces and one wordmark treatment

Both plates are drawn as SVG with turbulence, displacement, blur, vignette, and grain, then rendered to bitmaps. The source is in the repository at design/img/plates-source.html. Regenerate from there. Do not substitute photographs.

The wordmark as cut metal

Limelight in ivory, tracked at 0.035em, sits on a four-step grey extrusion with a long soft cast shadow down and to the right, matching the direction of the blinds light. A second copy of the wordmark clips the same blinds plate to the letters and multiplies onto the face at 70% opacity, so the light through the blinds falls across the letterforms. Keep the face flat. Do not add a gradient or a gloss.

04Type

Small caps recipe

The fonts ship no true small caps, so set them by hand: uppercase, size at 0.78 of the parent, letter-spacing 0.10em. Rail heads and the newsletter heading use a tighter 13px at 0.14em. Use these for kickers, navigation, the folio, and the colophon.

Measure and wrapping

  • Titles run to 640px and use text-wrap: balance. The lead title runs to 620px.
  • Summaries run to 600px and clamp to two lines. The lead summary clamps to three. Write them to fit.
  • Product names with hyphens use a non-breaking hyphen, so "CopyPaste‑2" never splits at a line end.
  • Dates are ISO in the ledger and rail, and spelled out in prose: "2026-07-28" beside the entry, "28 July 2026" in the strip.
  • The date sits on the kicker baseline. The docket sits on the title baseline.
05Layout

A ledger with a rail from 1100px

The page is 1180px wide. From 1100px it splits into the ledger on the left and a 280px rail on the right with a 56px gap. Below 1100px the rail drops under the ledger at 600px wide. Inside the ledger a fixed margin column carries the date and docket, one vertical hairline runs the full height of the list, and entries are separated by space alone.

Rail

Five modules in order: the newsletter card, counts for this page, one line from the record, dates to watch, and tags with counts across the archive. 07Rules

Do this, not that

Fourteen iterations and fifteen independent critiques settled these. Each rule on the right cost a point at some stage.

Do

  • Lead with the newest record, then keep every entry the same size in date order.
  • Use jade for rules, the active section, and hover.
  • Keep the one button bone. No colour on any control.
  • Reserve vermilion for the Bad Labs tag and its counts.
  • Set labels as hand-made small caps in the serif.
  • Keep dates and dockets in mono, in the margin, on their baselines.
  • Separate entries with space and the vertical hairline.
  • Fill the rail to the length of the feed, then stop.
  • Regenerate plates from the SVG source when they change.

Do not

  • Add a second tier, a hero, or a featured card.
  • Add ornaments: dinkus, monogram, seal, corner brackets, glyphs on tags.
  • Use gold, amber, or any warm accent.
  • Put green, jade, or vermilion on a button or field.
  • Put labels in chips, badges, or boxes.
  • Mix a fourth face in anywhere.
  • Draw rules between entries.
  • Show a constant tag such as "FCC" on every row.
  • Duplicate Subscribe in the strip.
  • Use photographs where the plates belong.

Texture

One grain layer at 8% opacity in screen blend, from an SVG turbulence filter, sits over the whole page and ignores the pointer. A radial vignette darkens the corners of the ground. The blinds and lamp plates carry their own grain. Do not stack more.
